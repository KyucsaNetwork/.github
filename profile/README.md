# KYUCSA Network

### What is KYUCSA?
A community of computing students at Kyambogo University offering technical support, collaborative engineering, and mentorship for students entering or currently navigating the tech industry. We operate in close collaboration with **Google Developer Student Clubs (GDSC Kyambogo)** and the **IEEE Computer Society**.

### What are our objectives?
Our main goal is to respond comprehensively to the technical and academic needs of students. We aim to create a vast, compact network where students can freely collaborate and support each other. By bringing together diverse skill sets, we bridge the gap between academic theory and practical software engineering, sharing our experiences and knowledge in a collaborative environment.

### What do we build and study?
The resources and repositories provided by KYUCSA reflect the technical demands of our community. Our collaborative focus areas include:

* **Software Engineering:** Building scalable applications, admin dashboards, and robust web platforms using modern frameworks like **ReactJS** and **Laravel**.
* **Networking & Infrastructure:** Practical systems administration, featuring custom **MikroTik** router configurations for hotspot services and campus network management.
* **Computer Science Theory:** Collaborative study and problem-solving around the mathematical foundations of computation, including **Operations Research**, graph theory, and **Formal Language Theory** (finite automata, context-free grammars).

*(Note: Information from our weekly sessions and bootcamps is archived in our `docs` repository).*
